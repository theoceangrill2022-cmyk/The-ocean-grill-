Welcome

Good food. Good drinks. Good Goa.

The Ocean Grill Kitchen & Bar is a casual dining destination in Calangute, Goa, created for guests who want flavour, comfort and a lively atmosphere in one place.

From Goan favourites to English-inspired dishes, Chinese selections and fast-food classics, our menu brings together familiar favourites and bold flavours.

Come for the food, stay for the drinks, music, atmosphere and relaxed Goa experience.
